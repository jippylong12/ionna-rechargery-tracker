HOW TO READ THIS

IONNA does not include launch dates in its map data. The first collection is a neutral baseline. “Newly observed” means first seen after that baseline, and “observed openings” means this tracker saw a site move from a non-open status to open. These are observation dates—not claimed construction or opening dates.
